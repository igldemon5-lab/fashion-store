FASHION STORE — WEBSITE STARTER

Files:
- index.html: mobile-friendly storefront with Men/Women filters and WhatsApp order links.
- privacy.html: simple privacy notice template.
- terms.html: simple terms/returns template.

BEFORE PUBLISHING:
1. Open index.html in a text/code editor.
2. Find 91XXXXXXXXXX and replace it with your WhatsApp number including country code, digits only. Example format: 919876543210.
3. Edit STORE_NAME and the products array (names, categories, prices, descriptions).
4. Replace emoji product placeholders with real product photos if you know how to edit the HTML; otherwise ask for help.
5. Read and customize privacy/terms pages to match your actual business and local laws.
6. Test every WhatsApp link on your phone.

Important:
- Prices and products are sample placeholders, not real stock.
- This site does not take online payments. Orders are confirmed by WhatsApp.
- Do not publish your personal address. Use a business contact method and share customer data only as needed to fulfil orders.
- If under 18, ask a parent/guardian to help with payments, accounts, shipping, and legal/tax requirements.
- Free static hosting is possible with GitHub Pages, but it does not include a custom domain by default.
